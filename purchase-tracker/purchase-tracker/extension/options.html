Purchase Tracker

Every 3 hours while Chrome is open, checks Facebook order status (tracking + seller), Meta Pay / Marketplace notifications (cancellations) and USPS tracking pages for items in your Purchased tab. Eligible items are moved to eBay inventory by Google Apps Script.

Cancellation signals

Each run (only while you have active purchases) also reads your Meta Pay wallet and Facebook Marketplace notifications to catch Canceled / Refunded / Expired orders. Meta Pay rows show the seller's name, so they are matched to purchases by the Seller column, verified by purchase date and amount.

Probe a Facebook listing

Verifies the "View Order" → "Order status" extraction (status, tracking number, seller) on a purchase you actually made. Opens the page in a background tab, shows exactly what was found, and sends nothing to your sheet. Run this on a few real orders (awaiting shipment, shipped, delivered, canceled) before trusting the automation.

Output appears here. The text preview may contain your address — it stays on this screen.

Probe Meta Pay wallet

Opens your Meta Pay wallet in a background tab and lists the transactions it could read (seller, status, date, amount), plus a read-only preview of which Purchased rows the backend would update. Nothing is written. If Meta asks for your password, this reports REAUTH_REQUIRED — open the wallet once by hand.

Output appears here.

Probe Marketplace notifications

Reads Marketplace notifications and lists the order events it recognised (canceled / refunded / expired / delivered …), plus a read-only preview of matching Purchased rows. Nothing is written.

Output appears here.

Probe a USPS tracking number

Opens tools.usps.com/tracking in a background tab, types the number in, submits, and shows what was read. Nothing is sent to your sheet.
