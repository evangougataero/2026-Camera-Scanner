Purchase Tracker

Probe a Facebook listing

Verifies the Order Details extraction on a purchase you actually made. Opens the page in a background tab, shows exactly what was found, and sends nothing to your sheet. Run this on a few real orders (awaiting shipment, shipped, canceled) before trusting the automation.

Output appears here. The text preview may contain your address — it stays on this screen.

Probe a USPS tracking number

Opens tools.usps.com/tracking in a background tab, types the number in, submits, and shows what was read. Nothing is sent to your sheet.

Output appears here.
